What we do

From the first sketch to the final coat of finish.

We handle the whole job, so there's one team responsible for the result, and the woodwork is built by the same people who install it.

General Contracting

Complete management of residential and commercial builds: permits, scheduling, trades and inspections, all handled by us.

Custom Millwork

Our story

A family name on every job.

Gaul Brothers Construction started in Union City, New Jersey, with two brothers who had grown up in the trades and wanted to run their own work. We still run it that way: the people who quote your project are the ones who show up to build it.

Over time, the finish work became what clients asked us for most. So we brought millwork in-house, and today we design, fabricate and install custom woodwork alongside our general contracting. That combination has made us a trusted partner for restaurant work, from multi-location national chains to some of New York City's high-end dining rooms.
